## Interpretation

* **No Egyptian official factor is recorded for 2025.** The FY2023/24 EEHC intensity is *not*
  copied forward: the specification explicitly forbids presenting older-year data as actual 2025
  data.
* The 12 international-default entries (six combustion fuels, clinker, five refrigerant/GWP values)
  are usable as clearly-labelled fallbacks — they are not Egyptian figures.
* The 8 pending activities are: grid consumption factor, thermal intensity 2025, landfill,
  incineration, wastewater, freight tonne-km, agriculture (enteric), AFOLU (forestry).

## What would change this assessment

1. Publication of the EEHC Annual Report FY2024/25 → enables the thermal-intensity factor for
   2025 (entry would become Available after reviewer verification).
2. A verified Egypt-specific grid factor (IEA/Ember/official) → closes the largest gap
   (Scope 2 consumer factor).
3. EEAA/CAPMAS 2025 inventory data → enables the waste/agriculture methodology parameters.

Until then, any 2025 calculation using a non-Egyptian source is visibly labelled by classification
and requires explicit user confirmation.
